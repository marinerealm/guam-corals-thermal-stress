---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Sclerophytum sp. has n = 2 biological replicates per group.
 2. Acropora Endozoicomonas Cq values (33-35) are at or beyond the
    limit of quantification; samples AC2 and AC3 did not amplify.
    Do not report a fold change for this comparison.
 3. Acropora Durusdinium Cq values (32-33) are near the LOQ and
    replicate variation was high (fold change 2.3 to 11.5).
 4. Two technical replicates per sample, not three.
 5. Amplification efficiencies were assumed equal across assays
    (Livak method). State this explicitly if standard curves were
    not run.
 6. For Endozoicomonas the parametric ANOVA (p = 0.013) and the
    non-parametric Kruskal-Wallis (p = 0.073) disagree. This reflects
    low power at small n. Report both.
